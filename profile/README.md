# ElementeX Labs Inc.

ElementeX explores environmental technology in energy, water, monitoring and climate adaptation, and builds data analysis for utility asset management. Its public work so far is three reproducible studies and demonstrations on public and synthetic utility and sewer data, including a study of 15,873 public transformer records that found no clear AUC winner between its two failure-ranking models. Public materials may include research models, data methods and concept designs. They do not establish measured environmental savings or field-certified infrastructure.

## Open by design

**We build in the open.** ElementeX Labs Inc. publishes its research code and methods under open licences (usually Apache-2.0 for code and CC BY 4.0 for written material), so public-interest teams can use them, check how they work and adapt them freely. Open work is easier to trust, because anyone can see exactly how a result is produced.

**Our own work, and only ours.** Everything we publish here is our own, built from public data and synthetic examples. It never includes client data, client projects, or anyone else's confidential information or intellectual property.

## Public projects

- [asset-health-demo](https://github.com/elementexlabs/asset-health-demo): a method demonstration on invented data for 300 transformers and 700 poles, showing data quality checks, inventory and replacement value, a health index, an age profile, replacement scenarios and four acceptance checks. It runs in the browser at https://elementexlabs.ca/demos/asset-health/.
